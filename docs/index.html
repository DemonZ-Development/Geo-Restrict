GeoRestrict

Geographic restriction plugin for Minecraft servers

Developed by Demonz Development

Bukkit Paper Spigot BungeeCord Waterfall Velocity Folia

Supports Minecraft 1.16 — 1.26.x

Features

🌍

Country Blocking

Allowlist or blocklist entire countries. Control exactly which regions can access your server with flexible country-code rules.

🛡️

VPN & Proxy Detection

Detect VPNs, proxies, and hosting providers automatically. Keep your server safe from anonymous connections.

📡

ASN / ISP Filtering

Block or allow specific ISPs and ASNs. Fine-grained control over which networks can reach your server.

💬

Discord Webhooks

Get notified instantly when players are blocked. Rich Discord embeds with player info, IP, country, and reason.

⚡

Local Privacy Cache

Cache lookup results locally on your server. No data is sent to or stored on any third-party cloud service.

🔄

Auto Updates

Silent update checking via Modrinth. Stay up to date without any manual effort — get notified of new releases.
